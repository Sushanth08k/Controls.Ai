DATA RETENTION AND ARCHIVAL POLICY #POL-FIN-2024-001

1. Scope and Target:
This policy establishes mandatory data retention guidelines for the core banking database entity 'transactions'. All records located in source table 'transactions' with date identified by 'txn_date' must be governed according to this standard.

2. Retention Horizon:
Records must be retained actively for a period of 7 years from transaction execution date. Upon reaching the 7 years retention threshold, records shall be archived and deleted ('archive_then_delete'). Archived records must be deposited into destination 'transactions_archive'.

3. Mandatory Exclusions:
Accounts subject to active regulatory preservation ('legal_hold') or ongoing fraud investigations are strictly excluded from archival and deletion actions until such holds are formally released.
